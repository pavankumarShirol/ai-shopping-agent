# E-commerce FAQ

This document contains frequently asked questions and answers for customers using our e-commerce platform.

---

## 1. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods including credit cards, debit cards, net banking, UPI, and digital wallets. Cash on Delivery (COD) may also be available for selected locations.

---

## 2. How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link via email or SMS. You can also track your order by logging into your account and visiting the **My Orders** section.

---

## 3. What is your return policy?

Most products can be returned within **7 days of delivery** if they are unused and in their original packaging. Some items such as personal care products and customized items may not be eligible for return.

---

## 4. How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes **3–7 business days** depending on your location. Remote areas may take slightly longer.

---

## 5. Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Yes, you can cancel your order before it is shipped. Go to the **My Orders** section, select the order, and click **Cancel Order**.

---

## 6. Do you offer refunds?

Yes, once the returned product is received and inspected, the refund will be processed within **5–7 business days** to the original payment method.

---

## 7. How can I change my delivery address?

You can change the delivery address before the order is shipped by going to **My Orders → Edit Address**. Once the order is shipped, the address cannot be modified.

---

## 8. Are there any shipping charges?

Shipping is **free for orders above ₹999**. For orders below this amount, a standard delivery charge may apply depending on the location.

---

## 9. What should I do if I receive a damaged or wrong product?

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact our support team within **48 hours of delivery**. You can raise a request in the **Help Center** or from the **My Orders** page.

---

## 10. Do I need an account to place an order?

Yes, you need to create an account to place an order so that you can track orders, manage returns, and receive updates about your purchases.

---
